Rules:
1. Every action, line, voiceover, and environment detail must be supported by the clip.
2. An action with origin source must be an ordered excerpt of the source. An action with origin bridge may only complete a necessary, visible, non-spoken connective action for an event explicitly established in the source, and must cite exact source excerpts in evidence.
3. A bridge may expand an established event into a continuous filmable process. 8. Preserve unquoted direct speech exactly. Do not add quotation marks, rewrite a line, or put narrative explanation into a character's lip performance.
